🛡️ Gap 2: Trust, False Alarm Reduction & Explainable AI (SHAP)

Traditional ICU monitors alarm on every temporary vital fluctuation, causing severe alarm fatigue. Gap 2 uses a second-stage LightGBM trust filter to eliminate false alarms and SHAP to explain why true alarms fire.

Current bedside monitors trigger on isolated snapshot thresholds. When patients cough, roll over, or have loose lead sensors, noisy alarms fire constantly. Clinicians suffer severe alarm fatigue, turn monitor volumes down, or silence alerts — leading to missed fatal arrests.

The second-stage LightGBM validity filter scores each candidate alert using trend slopes and shock indices. Spurious spikes are silently suppressed, while true imminent deteriorations are escalated with transparent SHAP clinical reasons.
